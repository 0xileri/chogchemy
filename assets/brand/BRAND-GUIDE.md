# Chogchemy · Brand kit v1

**Two Chogs. One new character.**

Chogchemy is the Chog fusion studio: use your Chog and another Chog to create a new character, with a record of its NFT roots and holder approvals.

## Identity

Playful, curious, expressive. A small comic studio with an experimental streak. Use **Chogchemy** in prose and **chogchemy** in the logo. Pronunciation: “chog-kem-ee”.

The emblem brings two rounded forms together around a four-point spark. Lime and pink represent the two parent references; the spark represents their new character. It is a new Chogchemy symbol, not official Chog collection artwork.

## Colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink green | `#243024` | Text, outlines, primary buttons |
| Fusion lime | `#D9F478` | Main accent, result cards, highlights |
| Comic pink | `#E5A3B7` | Second-parent accent, illustrations |
| Warm cream | `#F7F6ED` | Main background |
| Studio sage | `#EEF3DF` | Cards and quiet surfaces |

Use cream as the main canvas, ink for readability, and lime/pink as accents. Put ink text on light colours; avoid white text on lime or pink. The PNGs contain rendered colour variations; these hex values are the exact UI colour tokens.

## Typography

- **Space Grotesk:** headings and short labels; medium/bold weights.
- **DM Sans:** body copy, controls, captions; regular/medium weights.
- The logo has its own generated lettering. Use the supplied image instead of rebuilding the wordmark with a font.

## Ready-to-use copy

**Tagline:** Two Chogs. One new character.

**Descriptor:** The Chog fusion studio.

**Social bio:** Fuse your Chog with another. Create a new character and discover its roots. Built for Chogathon.

**One-line pitch:** Chogchemy turns your Chog and another Chog into a new AI character, with artist-guided style and a record of its NFT roots.

**Welcome:** A little of yours. A little of theirs. Meet someone new.

**Action labels:** Prepare fusion · Create our character · Meet your new character · Explore the family.

**Approval language:** Creator verified · Partner has not signed · Partner signed · Both holders signed. Match the wording to the actual approval record. Referencing another Chog does not mean its holder endorsed the result.

Describe generated results as characters or artwork. The current app does not mint new NFTs. Say “AI-generated” when describing the generation process, and credit the comic artist for their original style references when included.

## Asset use

- `chogchemy-logo-v1.png`: transparent primary logo. Use on cream or another very light background. Keep its proportions; leave clear space roughly equal to the central spark on every side. Use at least 180 px wide on screen.
- `chogchemy-icon-v1.png`: transparent square emblem for app icons, profile images, and stickers. Use on a light background. The square includes safe padding; preview circular crops before posting.
- `chogchemy-banner-v1.png`: 3:1 social header with logo, tagline, and emblem. Upload as a header/banner; keep all text visible when cropping.

These are high-resolution raster PNGs, not editable vector masters. Use the logo image directly; obtain a carefully redrawn vector master for large print runs.

## Comic artist direction

Build on expressive linework, rounded silhouettes, thick ink outlines, simple shading, and readable faces. Combine recognizable traits from both parent Chogs into one coherent character. Use cream backgrounds for character showcases; use lime, pink, and sparse halftone accents for supporting graphics. Avoid crowded collages and dense decorative backgrounds.

The artist supplies original drawings and style notes, and reviews generated characters. These brand images were created with the built-in image generation tool; they are not the partner's artwork and are not automatically added as generation references.
