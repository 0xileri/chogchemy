## Description to paste

Chogchemy turns your verified Chog NFT into a recurring character for shareable memes, with a public passport that keeps its creative history as ownership changes.

Choose your actual Chog, pick a popular meme scene, select the role and write a joke. Ten recommended formats have two to four named caption boxes and complete suggestions; all 100 source templates remain searchable. AI redraws the scene using the exact NFT and two original artist style references. Captions are added locally, so their spelling is exact. The original creator can export different captions from the saved scene without another AI request or drawing allowance.

The NFT supplies a specific character and a stable transferable identity. The server verifies ownership before preparation and again immediately before generation. The passport groups appearances by token, preserving each original creator's credit. A later verified holder can continue that same character with fresh creations. Without the NFT, there is no authoritative holder or transferable character identity for that continuation. This scrapbook is stored by the app, not on-chain, and does not alter NFT metadata or transfer copyright.

A weekly creative prompt gives holders something new to make together. Entry is optional and explicit. Its wall shows only completed real memes opted into that prompt, with an honest empty state when no one has entered. The separate family studio also creates a child from two Chogs, family comics, subsequent episodes and chat stickers, with optional partner approval and public parent records.

Start at https://chogchemy.up.railway.app/examples without a wallet. It shows two caption exports from real saved AI scenes and a saved family comic. These exports preserve the original public records and do not count as new AI generations or users. Then inspect https://chogchemy.up.railway.app/chogs/1786 and the weekly prompt at https://chogchemy.up.railway.app/community. Holder testing instructions are in docs/JUDGE-GUIDE.md.

Built by @0xileri; original artist drawings and art direction by @theejhay01. AI produces final scenes. Existing Chog NFTs stay in their wallets; outputs are images, not newly minted NFTs. The stack is HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Node.js, SQLite on Railway, viem, Sharp/Pango and Orbio. Coding assistants were used.

Pre-existing work: the initial wallet, ownership/fusion and image-generation scaffold was committed on 8 October, before the current official 9 October start. Later work added branding, public pages, family features, the meme studio, caption exports, passports and weekly prompts. The existing collection, Imgflip snapshot, image model and open-source libraries supply infrastructure. The two hand-drawn style references were supplied by our artist; their creation dates still need confirmation. AI-assisted branding and procedural test fixtures are disclosed. Fixtures are excluded from production and community/passport history.

Builder testing and review of two real meme scenes improved progress messages, request sizes, saved-result navigation and captions. Independent holder feedback, artist approval of the curated list, broad real-output quality and retention are not yet established. Next we will test more Chog designs, improve layouts from feedback, and measure actual return use rather than count stated intentions. There is no $CHOG or $CHMY spending integration.

## Judge access instructions

Public review needs no wallet or image request: open /examples, /chogs/1786, /community and an original saved meme page. Full creation needs a Chog Genesis holder wallet on Monad mainnet. Live drawing allowance is four dispatched attempts per accountable holder and ten globally per UTC day, shared with family features. Failed dispatched attempts count.
